### Medium

- **Provider-state preservation scan has no aggregate resource bound** (`scripts/probe-cursor-wake-surfaces.mjs:522`)
  - Issue: `snapshotProviderState` fingerprints every top-level entry under
    both provider roots, resetting the 512-entry and 64 MiB limits for each
    entry. Cleanup fingerprints every pre-existing entry again. Because this
    work occurs before the provider process timeout begins, an installation
    with many individually in-bounds entries can force unbounded aggregate I/O
    and delay or prevent the supposedly bounded release probe from reaching
    provider execution.
  - Fix: Apply a shared aggregate entry, byte, and/or elapsed-time budget across
    both roots and the post-run preservation check, failing visibly before
    provider launch when the budget is exceeded. Add a regression test with
    enough individually in-bounds entries to exceed the aggregate budget.
  - Requirement: FR9, NFR5, NFR6
