- **Wake authorization can still expire during the final validation pipeline before CAS** (`skills/session-observer-collab/scripts/lib/runtime-adapter.mjs:349`)
  - Issue: Both owner hooks refresh the clock after `cursorUpdate()`, but then
    call `claimAdapterTrigger()` with that scalar timestamp
    (`skills/session-observer-collab/scripts/hooks/cursor-stop.mjs:276`,
    `skills/session-observer-collab/scripts/hooks/codex-stop.mjs:290`).
    `claimAdapterTrigger()` subsequently awaits `inspectAdapterLease()` and
    `validateCursorUpdate()` at lines 355 and 369, then invokes
    `compareAndSwapTrigger()` with the earlier `invocation.now` at line 385.
    Those awaited path, file-identity, and prefix validations—and lock
    acquisition inside the CAS—can cross the wait deadline or lease expiry
    after the hook's refresh. The CAS then evaluates `effectiveLease()` against
    the stale time and may spend continuation budget and emit a wake after
    authorization ended. The new deadline tests advance time in
    `beforeCursorUpdate`, before the existing refresh, so they do not cover
    expiry during these later awaits.
  - Fix: Make the trigger CAS obtain a fresh clock value after acquiring the
    lease lock, or pass a clock callback through `claimAdapterTrigger()` and
    evaluate it immediately before mutation under the lock. Do not treat a
    pre-validation scalar as authorization. Add both-owner regressions that
    cross the wait deadline and lease expiry during final continuity validation
    and/or CAS lock contention, asserting no wake, no cursor/budget change, and
    the correct idle/expired diagnostic.
  - Requirement: FR8, NFR1, NFR5; `p08-t23`

### Important

    and every candidate to `candidates` (lines 815-895), while each
    `cursorCandidate()` invokes whole-body `candidateDerivedFields()` before
    ranking. The `p08-t24` regression proves only that an explicit pin skips
    unrelated bodies; it does not cover the generic public discovery path

    rather than a silently partial exact result. Add a public unpinned
