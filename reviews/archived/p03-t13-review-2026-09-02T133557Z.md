Alternatively, make `observedId` classify only numeric nonzero codes as `provider-nonzero-exit` and route a null/no-signal/no-timeout result to `provider-call-exception`. Add a default-adapter or equivalent boundary test for `ENOENT` plus a null-exit/no-signal fixture; continue discarding raw error text and fields.
