Add synthetic regressions for unreadable direct and fallback transcript roots, failed project-root enumeration, and transcript-stat failure under `recency: 'exact-all'`; also prove the guidance layer reports `discovery-incomplete` and returns no partial candidates. Regenerate every affected runtime output from canonical TypeScript and apply the required version bumps to each changed canonical skill.
